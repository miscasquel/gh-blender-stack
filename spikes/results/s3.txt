# S3 — geometry round trip at 1k, 10k and 100k triangles

2026-10-11. Blender 5.2.2 (extraction and apply, headless), C# under wine
(Rhino Mesh conversion, live GH session), transport over the spike WebSocket.

**Result: PASS.** Geometry matches exactly in both directions at every size
(verts, face counts, face indices, colours), and the 100k round trip is well
inside the plan's ~100 ms budget.

## Live run (the C# side and the transport)

From the driver's session with the spike `.gha` in a live Rhino session:

| Case | Triangles | Frame | RTT | C# sections→Mesh | C# Mesh→sections |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1k | 968 | 0.02 MB | 5.6 ms | 2.0 ms | 1.7 ms |
| 10k | 10,082 | 0.18 MB | 4.1 ms | 1.1 ms | 0.7 ms |
| 100k | 100,352 | 1.61 MB | 30.3 ms | 10.0 ms | 5.1 ms |

RTT covers the request, both conversions, and the reply. The 1k case is slower
than the 10k case: it runs first and pays JIT warmup. The S1 path was rechecked
in the same session (PASS, 33.9 ms) because the spike `.gha` now speaks the real
frame codec.

```
1k:   reply 5.6 ms rtt; C# in 2.0 ms, out 1.7 ms;  529 verts,   484 faces
10k:  reply 4.1 ms rtt; C# in 1.1 ms, out 0.7 ms; 5184 verts,  5041 faces
100k: reply 30.3 ms rtt; C# in 10.0 ms, out 5.1 ms; 50625 verts, 50176 faces
```

## Blender side (headless, from scripts/test-blender)

| Case | Extract + hash | Encode | Decode | Apply (rebuild) | Apply (in-place) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10k | 0.9 ms | 0.1 ms | 0.7 ms | 8.1 ms | covered by the tests |
| 100k | 7.4 ms | 0.3 ms | 6.6 ms | 99.9 ms | covered by the tests |

The apply figure is the worst case: `clear_geometry` + `from_pydata`, used when
the topology changed. When only positions move, `foreach_set` updates in place,
which is the engine's normal path (bench_geo measured 22 ms at 100k).

## Budget

Plan §13 says "geometry hop at 100k triangles: about 100 ms of transport and
conversion on top of the GH solve". Measured: **30.3 ms** for the whole round
trip (both conversions and both directions of transport, 1.61 MB each way).
Blender's own read+encode at 100k adds 7.7 ms, and the in-place apply is ~22 ms.
The budget holds with room to spare; no revision needed.

## Notes and defects found on the way

- WebSocket frames over 64 KiB use a 64-bit length. The spike client rejected
  them on receive (fixed: big-endian 64-bit read) and then encoded them wrongly
  on send (fixed: shifting an `int` by 32 wraps to 0 in C#, so the length's high
  half repeated its low half). The WsTest pre-flight now moves a 200,000 byte
  frame in both directions.
- RhinoCommon's `Mesh.Vertices.AddVertices` cannot be referenced with the Mono
  profile mcs compiles against (`IReadOnlyList` missing), so the conversion adds
  vertices one at a time; the 10 ms at 100k includes that.
- The conversion runs on the socket thread here to measure it cleanly; M4 will
  convert during `SolveInstance` instead (plan §10).
