  Blender's own evaluation: `knots[i] = i` in Rhino reproduces Blender's uniform
  spline exactly (both curves start at control point 1 and end at n-2).
- The circle number matches S4's 0.73 measurement exactly.

## What this changes for the plugin

- The curve classification is settled: exact for clamped-uniform and uniform
  NURBS (orders 2..6, weights kept), Bezier nets for everything else, and the
  D3 warning is emitted with the measured deviation only when weights are lost.
- SubD creases transfer by vertex pair and survive the cage round trip; the
  fractional-value warning reports the affected edge count.
- The mixed-output orchestration (`SectionsFromGeometry`) is exercised live by
  Stage Out; its combining loops are covered by the individual paths above and
  by the layer B mixed-response test (`blender/tests/headless/test_geometry_kinds.py`).
